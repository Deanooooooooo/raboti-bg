# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and small teams in Bulgarian businesses who lose time or customer enquiries to repeated operational work. They are evaluating whether an AI employee can take over one bounded task without forcing them to replace the software they already use.

## Product Purpose

Raboti provides subscription AI employees for specific business jobs: answering calls, moving information between systems, maintaining websites, preparing accounting routine, and following up on enquiries. Success means one named recurring job is completed within agreed boundaries and handed to a person when judgment or approval is required.

## Positioning

Raboti sells AI as named, bounded job roles configured around the client's existing work, systems, approvals, and escalation rules rather than as a generic chatbot or automation builder.

## Operating Context

Clients work across phone calls, email, calendars, spreadsheets, CRMs, websites, online stores, booking tools, cloud storage, and internal APIs. Raboti's team configures the connections; the client grants the necessary access and approves risky actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current live roles are Vdiga, Pomaga, and Pishe; Prodava and Smyata are marked as upcoming.
- The integrations page may describe technically supported direct connections, but must not imply they are already active for every customer.
- The first-wave integration set excludes Zapier and Make because they add client setup, billing, and maintenance burden.
- Preferred first-wave connections are Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot, Pipedrive, WordPress, WooCommerce, Calendly, Cal.com, Slack notifications, and documented custom APIs/webhooks.
- Connections with significant platform review, partner access, enterprise configuration, or unverified merchant access must be marked as requiring administrator approval or technical review.
- Payments, contracts, publishing, and accounting writes require human approval by default.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Работи. Public copy is natural Bulgarian, concrete, and avoids em dashes. The existing site identity and interface are the visual authority for new routes.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains product descriptions, prices, boundaries, FAQs, company details, and the current site implementation. Official developer documentation confirms APIs for the selected first-wave systems. No customer counts, integration usage counts, testimonials, or one-click-install claims are available and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Start from a recognizable piece of work, not from the technology.
- Connect to the client's current software when a supported, secure path exists.
- Make access, approvals, and handoff boundaries visible before setup.
- Prefer direct maintained connections over client-built automation chains.
- State uncertainty and technical-review requirements before making a promise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public web experience must remain keyboard accessible, readable on mobile, compatible with reduced motion, and understandable without relying on color alone.
