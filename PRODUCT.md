# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary buyer is the owner or manager of a Bulgarian small business without a dedicated operations or IT team. They are currently losing time or customers to missed calls, outdated website content, or repeated copying of data between tools.

## Product Purpose

Работи provides scoped AI employees that take over a defined recurring business role. Success means a buyer can identify a real task, understand which role can take it, see the monthly price and boundaries, and request an evaluation without needing technical expertise.

## Positioning

Работи does not sell generic AI software. It sells a named, scoped role with a monthly price, explicit permissions, human-approval boundaries, and a record of completed work.

## Operating Context

Work begins from real inputs such as incoming calls, email, forms, documents, schedules, spreadsheets, websites, and CRM systems. Before setup, the customer shows how the task is currently performed. The role is then limited to approved information, systems, and actions; irreversible actions remain subject to human approval.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The products currently available are Вдига, Пише, and Помага.
- Вдига handles incoming calls and appointment-related conversations.
- Пише handles scoped website-content work.
- Помага handles recurring operational work between systems.
- Payments, contracts, publishing, and other irreversible actions require human approval where stated by the service.
- Смята and Продава must not be presented as available products.
- Existing routes, prices, contact flows, legal pages, and structured product data are factual constraints and must remain accurate.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Работи.
- Language: clear, direct Bulgarian for non-technical business owners.
- AI employees are described through concrete jobs, permissions, prices, and outcomes—not as generic AI technology.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains product descriptions, prices, role pages, process explanations, FAQs, contact flows, and company/legal information.
- There are no approved customer logos, testimonials, case studies, or performance metrics. Future work must not fabricate any of them.
- Product demonstrations may be illustrative only when clearly labeled as examples rather than customer evidence.

## Product Principles

- Start from the customer’s real unfinished work, not from AI features.
- Make the role, scope, price, and human boundary legible before asking for contact.
- Demonstrate how work moves from input to recorded result.
- Distinguish available roles from future roles without ambiguity.
- Prefer honest product evidence over invented social proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public website must remain keyboard accessible, responsive, and usable by Bulgarian-speaking visitors without technical knowledge.
